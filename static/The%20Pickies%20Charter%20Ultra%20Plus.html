The Pickies Charter
  1. Every year we play three games and predict what will happen at WWDC, what will happen during the autumnal season, and over the course of the entire year.
  2. James always picks first.John then flips a coin to see who goes second.
  3. For the Autumnal Pickies, everyone picks five things that they think will happen between September 1st and our Annual Pickies episode at the end of the year. James gets an additional sixth pick because he does just plain deserves it.
  4. In the case of a tie, there are multiple winners, unless one of those winners is James, in which case he wins by default.
  5. There shall be no disputes over scoring because we are all just inherently honorable people, especially James, who is the most honorable.
  6. At the end of each round, Jason and John, speaking in the third person about myself now, will alternate saying something nice about James.
  7. James will say thank you very much, and then we move on to the next round.
